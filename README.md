# Morning Briefings

Daily market intelligence, news, and personal updates delivered at 7:30 AM CST.

## What's Inside

Each briefing covers:
- **Markets:** NYSE, NASDAQ, Russell 2000, DJIA, Crude, Silver, Gold, BTC + crypto market cap
- **Financial News:** Top story of the day
- **Economic Calendar:** Most anticipated data release
- **Interest Topics:** AI/tech, oil & gas, Texas Rangers, UFO/UAP, Egyptology
- **Personal:** Birthdays & anniversaries
